# Honeypots y Tecnología de Decepción

> Decoy systems, fake services, and deception frameworks designed to detect, lure, deflect, or study unauthorized activity by adversaries.
> Sistemas de engodo, serviços simulados e frameworks de decepção para detectar, atrair e analisar ações não autorizadas de adversários.
> Sistemas señuelo, servicios simulados y marcos de engaño para detectar, atraer y analizar acciones no autorizadas de adversarios.

---

### [Cowrie](https://github.com/cowrie/cowrie) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cowrie/cowrie?style=social)](https://github.com/cowrie/cowrie)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Medium to high interaction SSH and Telnet honeypot designed to log brute force attacks and shell interaction performed by attackers.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Honeypot de média e alta interação para SSH e Telnet projetado para registrar ataques de força bruta e comandos executados por invasores.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Honeypot de media y alta interacción para SSH y Telnet diseñado para registrar ataques de fuerza bruta y comandos ejecutados por atacantes.

### [Dionaea](https://github.com/DinoTools/dionaea) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/DinoTools/dionaea?style=social)](https://github.com/DinoTools/dionaea)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Honeypot designed to trap malware exploiting network vulnerabilities (SMB, HTTP, FTP, TFTP) and automatically capture malicious payloads.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Honeypot projetado para capturar malwares que exploram vulnerabilidades de rede (SMB, HTTP, FTP) e armazenar os payloads maliciosos.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Honeypot diseñado para capturar malware que explota vulnerabilidades de red (SMB, HTTP, FTP) y almacenar las cargas maliciosas.

### [T-Pot](https://github.com/telekom-security/tpotce) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/telekom-security/tpotce?style=social)](https://github.com/telekom-security/tpotce)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** The all-in-one multi-honeypot platform by Deutsche Telekom running in Docker containers with integrated Elastic Stack dashboards.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** A plataforma definitiva multi-honeypot da Deutsche Telekom executada em containers Docker com dashboards integrados no Elastic Stack.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** La plataforma definitiva multi-honeypot de Deutsche Telekom que se ejecuta en contenedores Docker con paneles integrados en Elastic Stack.

### [Canarytokens](https://github.com/thinkst/canarytokens) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/thinkst/canarytokens?style=social)](https://github.com/thinkst/canarytokens)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Open-source digital tripwires (tokens, DNS, PDF, Word, SQL) that notify administrators whenever an intruder accesses or opens them.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Armadilhas digitais open source (tokens, DNS, PDF, Word, SQL) que notificam administradores imediatamente quando um invasor as acessa.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Señuelos digitales de código abierto (tokens, DNS, PDF, Word, SQL) que notifican a los administradores cuando un intruso los abre.

### [Heralding](https://github.com/johnnykv/heralding) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/johnnykv/heralding?style=social)](https://github.com/johnnykv/heralding)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Credentials-catching honeypot supporting multiple authentication protocols (FTP, Telnet, SSH, HTTP, HTTPS, POP3, IMAP, SMTP).
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Honeypot focado na captura de credenciais suportando múltiplos protocolos de autenticação (FTP, Telnet, SSH, HTTP, IMAP, SMTP).
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Honeypot centrado en la captura de credenciales con soporte para múltiples protocolos de autenticación (FTP, Telnet, SSH, HTTP, IMAP, SMTP).
