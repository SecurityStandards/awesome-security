# Essential Security Books & Canonical Academic Papers

> Foundational literature, seminal security publications, and classic reading materials across application security, cryptography, and network defense.
> Literatura fundamental, publicações seminais de segurança e leituras clássicas em segurança de aplicações, criptografia e defesa de redes.

---

## 📚 Essential Books / Livros Fundamentais / Libros Fundamentales

### [Threat Modeling: Designing for Security](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Threat+Modeling%3A+Designing+for+Security-p-9781118809990) - *Adam Shostack*
- The authoritative guide on how to build actionable threat models into software development pipelines.

### [The Web Application Hacker's Handbook: Finding and Exploiting Security Flaws](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/The+Web+Application+Hacker%27s+Handbook%3A+Finding+and+Exploiting+Security+Flaws%2C+2nd+Edition-p-9781118026472) - *Dafydd Stuttard & Marcus Pinto*
- The undisputed Bible of web application penetration testing, covering core technologies and practical exploit techniques.

### [Serious Cryptography: A Practical Introduction to Modern Encryption](https://nostarch.com/seriouscrypto) - *Jean-Philippe Aumasson*
- Practical, rigorous introduction to modern cryptographic primitives, symmetric ciphers, hashing, public keys, and TLS without getting lost in mathematical abstractions.

### [Designing Data-Intensive Applications: The Big Ideas Behind Reliable, Scalable, and Maintainable Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-data-intensive-applications/9781491903063/) - *Martin Kleppmann*
- The foundational textbook on distributed systems architecture, consistency, replication, and data integrity.

### [Practical Malware Analysis: The Hands-On Guide to Dissecting Malicious Software](https://nostarch.com/malware) - *Michael Sikorski & Andrew Honig*
- Comprehensive handbook for setting up isolated labs, debugging assembly code, and reverse engineering Windows binaries.

### [Blue Team Handbook: Incident Response Edition](https://www.amazon.com/Blue-Team-Handbook-condensed-Responder/dp/1500734756) - *Don Murdoch*
- Compact, field-tested reference guide for SOC analysts and incident responders responding to enterprise compromises.

## 📄 Seminal Papers / Artigos Canônicos / Artículos Seminales

### [Smashing The Stack For Fun And Profit](http://phrack.org/issues/49/14.html) - *Aleph One (Phrack Magazine, 1996)*
- The landmark paper that educated an entire generation of computer scientists on buffer overflows, stack frames, and arbitrary shellcode execution.

### [New Directions in Cryptography](https://ee.stanford.edu/~hellman/publications/pub024.pdf) - *Whitfield Diffie & Martin E. Hellman (IEEE, 1976)*
- The foundational academic publication that introduced asymmetric public-key cryptography and the Diffie-Hellman key exchange protocol.

### [A Method for Obtaining Digital Signatures and Public-Key Cryptosystems](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rivest/Rsapaper.pdf) - *R.L. Rivest, A. Shamir, & L. Adleman (Communications of the ACM, 1978)*
- The original paper presenting the RSA algorithm, combining modular arithmetic and prime factorization to establish digital signatures.

### [Reflections on Trusting Trust](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rdriley/487/papers/Thompson_1984_ReflectionsonTrustingTrust.pdf) - *Ken Thompson (Turing Award Lecture, 1984)*
- The Turing Award lecture demonstrating that you cannot trust code you did not totally create yourself, exposing supply chain compiler backdoor propagation.

### [The Protection of Information in Computer Systems](http://members.unine.ch/pascal.felber/cours/cs/papers/protection.pdf) - *Jerome H. Saltzer & Michael D. Schroeder (IEEE, 1975)*
- Seminal paper formulating the timeless principles of information security: Economy of mechanism, Fail-safe defaults, Complete mediation, and Least privilege.
