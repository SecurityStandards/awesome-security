# Honeypots & Deception Technology

> Decoy systems, fake services, and deception frameworks designed to detect, lure, deflect, or study unauthorized activity by adversaries.
> Sistemas de engodo, serviços simulados e frameworks de decepção para detectar, atrair e analisar ações não autorizadas de adversários.

---

### [Cowrie](https://github.com/cowrie/cowrie) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cowrie/cowrie?style=social)](https://github.com/cowrie/cowrie)
- Medium to high interaction SSH and Telnet honeypot designed to log brute force attacks and shell interaction performed by attackers.

### [Dionaea](https://github.com/DinoTools/dionaea) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/DinoTools/dionaea?style=social)](https://github.com/DinoTools/dionaea)
- Honeypot designed to trap malware exploiting network vulnerabilities (SMB, HTTP, FTP, TFTP) and automatically capture malicious payloads.

### [T-Pot](https://github.com/telekom-security/tpotce) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/telekom-security/tpotce?style=social)](https://github.com/telekom-security/tpotce)
- The all-in-one multi-honeypot platform by Deutsche Telekom running in Docker containers with integrated Elastic Stack dashboards.

### [Canarytokens](https://github.com/thinkst/canarytokens) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/thinkst/canarytokens?style=social)](https://github.com/thinkst/canarytokens)
- Open-source digital tripwires (tokens, DNS, PDF, Word, SQL) that notify administrators whenever an intruder accesses or opens them.

### [Heralding](https://github.com/johnnykv/heralding) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/johnnykv/heralding?style=social)](https://github.com/johnnykv/heralding)
- Credentials-catching honeypot supporting multiple authentication protocols (FTP, Telnet, SSH, HTTP, HTTPS, POP3, IMAP, SMTP).
