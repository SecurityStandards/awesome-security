### [Nmap](https://github.com/nmap/nmap) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nmap/nmap?style=social)](https://github.com/nmap/nmap)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** The foundational open-source network exploration tool, port scanner, and security scanner with extensive NSE scripting capabilities.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** A ferramenta fundamental open source de exploração de rede, varredura de portas e auditoria de segurança com scripts NSE.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** La herramienta fundamental de código abierto para exploración de redes, escaneo de puertos y auditoría de seguridad con scripts NSE.

### [Masscan](https://github.com/robertdavidgraham/masscan) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/robertdavidgraham/masscan?style=social)](https://github.com/robertdavidgraham/masscan)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** High-speed internet-scale port scanner transmitting SYN packets asynchronously, capable of scanning the entire public IPv4 space in minutes.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Scanner de portas assíncrono em escala de internet capaz de varrer todo o espaço público IPv4 em minutos.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Escáner de puertos asíncrono a escala de internet capaz de escanear todo el espacio público de IPv4 en minutos.

### [RustScan](https://github.com/RustScan/RustScan) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/RustScan/RustScan?style=social)](https://github.com/RustScan/RustScan)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Ultra-fast port scanner written in Rust that discovers open ports in seconds and pipes open targets directly into Nmap for deep enumeration.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Scanner de portas ultrarrápido em Rust que encontra portas abertas em segundos e repassa alvos ao Nmap para enumeração profunda.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Escáner de puertos ultrarrápido en Rust que descubre puertos abiertos en segundos y canaliza los objetivos directamente a Nmap.

### [Naabu](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/naabu) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/projectdiscovery/naabu?style=social)](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/naabu)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Fast, reliable port scanning tool written in Go with SYN/CONNECT scan support built specifically for fast attack surface discovery.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Scanner de portas rápido e confiável escrito em Go com suporte a varreduras SYN/CONNECT desenvolvido para descoberta ágil de superfície.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Escáner de puertos rápido y confiable escrito en Go con soporte de escaneo SYN/CONNECT para descubrimiento ágil de superficie.

### [Httpx](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/httpx) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/projectdiscovery/httpx?style=social)](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/httpx)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** Fast, modular HTTP probing toolkit designed to perform multiple parallel checks (status codes, titles, technologies, TLS data) on web hosts.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Toolkit rápido de sondagem HTTP modular para executar checagens paralelas (status codes, títulos, tecnologias, certificados) em massa.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Toolkit rápido de sondeo HTTP modular para ejecutar comprobaciones paralelas (códigos de estado, títulos, tecnologías, TLS) en masa.

### [Bettercap](https://github.com/bettercap/bettercap) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bettercap/bettercap?style=social)](https://github.com/bettercap/bettercap)
* 🇺🇸 **EN:** The Swiss Army knife for network attacks, MITM assessments, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), wireless, and Ethernet reconnaissance.
* 🇧🇷 **PT:** Ferramenta multifuncional completa para ataques de rede, avaliações MITM, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), redes wireless e Ethernet.
* 🇪🇸 **ES:** Herramienta multifuncional para ataques de red, evaluaciones MITM, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), redes inalámbricas y Ethernet.
