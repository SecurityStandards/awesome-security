# Threat Modeling, Risk Assessment & Security Architecture

> Methodologies, toolkits, and structured frameworks for identifying design flaws, calculating impact, and architecting secure systems.
> Metodologias, ferramentas e frameworks estruturados para identificar falhas de design, calcular impacto e projetar sistemas seguros.

---

### [OWASP Threat Dragon](https://github.com/owasp/threat-dragon) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/owasp/threat-dragon?style=social)](https://github.com/owasp/threat-dragon)
- Open-source, web-based and desktop threat modeling tool created by OWASP featuring diagram generation and STRIDE methodology.

### [PyTM](https://github.com/izar/pytm) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/izar/pytm?style=social)](https://github.com/izar/pytm)
- Python-based framework for programmatic Threat Modeling-as-Code, automatically generating Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs) and threat reports.

### [Google VSAQ](https://github.com/google/vsaq) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/google/vsaq?style=social)](https://github.com/google/vsaq)
- Interactive questionnaire application developed by Google to assess security postures, web applications, and risks of external third-party vendors.

### [STRIDE Methodology](https://owasp.org/www-community/Threat_Modeling_Process)
- Industry standard mnemonic for categorizing software threats: Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, and Elevation of Privilege.
