# Cryptography, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) & Secrets Management

> Cryptographic libraries, certificate authority tooling, mutual TLS managers, and secrets storage vaults.
> Bibliotecas criptográficas, ferramentas de autoridade certificadora (CA), mutual TLS e cofres de gestão de segredos.

---

### [Step-CA (Smallstep)](https://github.com/smallstep/certificates) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/smallstep/certificates?style=social)](https://github.com/smallstep/certificates)
- Private Certificate Authority (CA) and PKI toolkit for DevOps, securing internal networks, microservices, and devices with automated mTLS.

### [Vault (OpenBao)](https://github.com/openbao/openbao) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openbao/openbao?style=social)](https://github.com/openbao/openbao)
- Community-driven open-source software for managing secrets, certificates, sensitive tokens, and dynamic encryption keys across distributed systems.

### [Age](https://github.com/FiloSottile/age) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/FiloSottile/age?style=social)](https://github.com/FiloSottile/age)
- Simple, modern, and secure file encryption tool, format, and Go library featuring small explicit keys, no config, and UNIX composability.

### [SOPS (Secrets OPerationS)](https://github.com/getsops/sops) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getsops/sops?style=social)](https://github.com/getsops/sops)
- Simple and flexible editor of encrypted files supporting YAML, JSON, ENV, and INI formats with AWS KMS, GCP KMS, Azure Key Vault, and age.
